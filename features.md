# Vote Social — Persona-Driven Selling Features & Waitlist Collateral

This document defines the five target customer personas for **Vote Social** and prunes our feature set down to the high-impact **selling features** that directly motivate those personas to convert on the waitlist. Plumbing, verification mechanics, and back-office setup steps have been removed.

---

## Target Personas

- **Disengaged Dan (The Busy, Overwhelmed Voter)**: Wants to do his civic duty without spending hours researching obscure municipal or riding candidates. He needs a 2-minute summary of who is on his ballot, which candidates are backed by neighbors and groups he already trusts, and stress-free logistics on when and where to vote.
- **Political Peter (The Engaged Policy & Neighborhood Power-User)**: Follows local issues closely, debates transit and housing policy with neighbors, scrutinizes council voting records, asks candidates pointed questions on the public record, and proudly puts a lawn sign on his front yard.
- **Candidate Cathy (The Grassroots Candidate & Campaign Manager)**: Running without a six-figure party machine. She needs an affordable, high-trust channel to reach verified voters in her exact boundary, answer constituent questions once on the record, and turn online supporters into lawn signs and door-knocking volunteers.
- **Organiser Olive (The Community Coalition & Voting Block Leader)**: Leads a local advocacy group, tenant union, ratepayer association, or BIA. She wants to publish voter guides, prove to candidates how many verified voters in each ward back her organization's slate, and mobilize her members to the polls.
- **Journalist Jon (The Local Reporter & Civic Watchdog)**: Covers municipal and riding races on a tight deadline. He needs real-time data on which neighborhood issues are surging, an on-the-record archive of candidate stances and past votes, and embeddable riding summaries for his articles.

---

## 1. Instant Ballot Clarity & Low-Effort Voter Readiness
*Primary Personas: **Disengaged Dan**, **Political Peter***

- **1A — Instant Postal-to-Ballot Lookup** *(Disengaged Dan)*: Enter your postal code to immediately see every Municipal, Provincial, and Federal election on your ballot and who is running.
- **1B — "Who Controls What?" Issue Slicer** *(Disengaged Dan, Political Peter)*: Automatically connects everyday frustrations (*Housing*, *Transit*, *Healthcare*) to the exact level of government and local policy proposals that actually control them.
- **1C — "Match My Priorities" 60-Second Ballot Compass** *(Disengaged Dan)*: Answer a quick quiz on top local issues to rank candidates and community voter guides by how closely they match your household's priorities.
- **1D — Shareable Personal Ballot Card** *(Disengaged Dan, Political Peter)*: Generate a clean "My Ballot & Endorsements" cheat sheet to bring into the voting booth or share with family and group chats.

---

## 2. Candidate Track Records & Deep-Dive Comparison
*Primary Personas: **Political Peter**, **Disengaged Dan**, **Journalist Jon***

- **2A — Side-by-Side Policy Stance Matrix** *(Political Peter, Disengaged Dan)*: Compare every candidate's explicit stance (*Strong Support*, *Conditional Support*, *Opposes*) across standardized local policy proposals.
- **2B — Verified Council Roll-Call Voting History** *(Political Peter, Journalist Jon)*: Inspect how incumbents actually voted on past council motions alongside how challengers state they would have voted.
- **2C — Candidate Resume & Platform Vault** *(Political Peter, Journalist Jon)*: View full candidate CVs, professional backgrounds, financial disclosures, and official platform PDFs in one place.
- **2D — Bite-Sized Debate & Town Hall Clips** *(Disengaged Dan, Political Peter)*: Watch 30-second video/audio highlights from local debates indexed by issue rather sitting through two-hour recordings.

---

## 3. Verified Neighborhood Forum & Public Q&A
*Primary Personas: **Political Peter**, **Candidate Cathy**, **Journalist Jon***

- **3A — Postal-Verified Neighbor Forum** *(Political Peter)*: Discuss local issues in a feed restricted to verified residents in your postal zone—free from bots and out-of-riding trolls.
- **3B — Community-Ranked Local Agenda** *(Political Peter, Candidate Cathy)*: Upvote the neighborhood issues that matter most so candidates are forced to address top constituent priorities.
- **3C — On-the-Record Candidate Q&A** *(Political Peter, Candidate Cathy, Journalist Jon)*: Put direct questions to candidates and read highlighted, official responses linked to their policy platform.
- **3D — Candidate Response-Rate Scorecard** *(Political Peter, Journalist Jon)*: See which candidates actively answer constituent questions and which ones ignore top-voted neighborhood concerns.
- **3E — Verified Neighbor Testimonials** *(Disengaged Dan, Political Peter)*: Read real-world vouches from verified neighbors in your ward explaining why they personally trust a candidate.

---

## 4. Community Voter Guides & Voting Block Power
*Primary Personas: **Disengaged Dan**, **Organiser Olive**, **Political Peter***

- **4A — Trusted Community Voter Guides** *(Disengaged Dan, Political Peter)*: See which candidates are endorsed by the local housing coalitions, transit groups, unions, or business associations you already trust.
- **4B — Live Coalition vs. Independent Voter Counts** *(Organiser Olive, Candidate Cathy, Disengaged Dan)*: See real-time numbers of how many verified ward voters are aligned with each community organization versus independent voters.
- **4C — Scorecard & Endorsed Slate Publisher** *(Organiser Olive)*: Publish your organization's candidate grades and endorsed slate directly onto local voters' Election Summary and Candidate Profile screens.
- **4D — Pinned Organization Briefings & Mobilization Alerts** *(Organiser Olive)*: Broadcast policy town halls, endorsement announcements, and advance-poll reminders directly to opt-in subscribers in target wards.

---

## 5. 1-Click Grassroots Campaign & Supporter Actions
*Primary Personas: **Political Peter**, **Candidate Cathy***

- **5A — 1-Click Lawn Sign & Window Poster Request** *(Political Peter, Candidate Cathy)*: Voters request a lawn sign in one tap; campaigns receive a pre-verified, geo-tagged delivery queue sorted by street.
- **5B — Public & Anonymous Vote Pledging** *(Political Peter, Disengaged Dan, Candidate Cathy)*: Pledge your vote to build visible ward momentum for your candidate—either with a public supporter badge or anonymously.
- **5C — 1-Click Volunteer & Canvass Shift RSVP** *(Political Peter, Candidate Cathy)*: Discover and join local door-knocking runs and campaign events in your neighborhood with a single click.
- **5D — "Publish Once, Cite Everywhere" Candidate Survey Engine** *(Candidate Cathy)*: Answer standardized local policy questions once and have your stances automatically surfaced across relevant neighborhood threads.
- **5E — Opt-In Constituent Broadcasts** *(Candidate Cathy)*: Send targeted policy updates and town hall invites to verified voters in your riding who follow your campaign or your key issues.

---

## 6. Stress-Free Election Day Logistics & Civic Calendar
*Primary Personas: **Disengaged Dan**, **Political Peter**, **Organiser Olive***

- **6A — Personalized Civic & Debate Calendar** *(Political Peter, Disengaged Dan)*: One weekly/monthly calendar combining Advance Poll windows, Election Day hours, and local events from the candidates and groups you follow.
- **6B — Assigned Polling Station Walking Map & ID Guide** *(Disengaged Dan)*: Get walking directions to your exact assigned polling place and a clear checklist of what ID to bring so you are never turned away.
- **6C — Zero-Spam Notification Controls** *(Disengaged Dan, Political Peter)*: Opt into essential Election Day and advance-poll reminders while keeping campaign updates silenced or bundled in a weekly digest.

---

## 7. Real-Time Civic Intelligence & Press Tools
*Primary Personas: **Journalist Jon**, **Candidate Cathy**, **Organiser Olive***

- **7A — Riding-by-Riding Issue Velocity Heatmaps** *(Journalist Jon, Candidate Cathy, Organiser Olive)*: Track which local issues and policy proposals are gaining the fastest upvote momentum among verified voters in each ward.
- **7B — On-the-Record Candidate Quote & Flip-Flop Archive** *(Journalist Jon, Political Peter)*: Search a timestamped record of every candidate's survey stances, forum answers, and past council roll-call votes.
- **7C — Embeddable Live Riding Summary Widgets** *(Journalist Jon)*: Drop interactive candidate comparison and top-issue summary cards directly into local news articles, Substack posts, and newsletters.
